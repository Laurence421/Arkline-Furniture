FAQs & terms

Good to know before you order.

Still unsure? Message us and we'll walk you through it.

Ask a question
How do I request the updated furniture pricelist?

Message us by email or on our Facebook page, or use the form below. Tell us what furniture you need and we'll send the current pricelist.

How do I make an order?

Ask for a price, approve the specifications, then pay the 70% down payment to confirm. Production starts after that.

How do I make a payment?

A 70% down payment confirms all orders. The remaining balance is paid before delivery unless otherwise agreed. Payment details come with your price.

Can I change my order after confirming?

Yes, but changes after confirmation may affect the price and delivery schedule.

Can I cancel a customized order?

Customized and made-to-order items are non-refundable and non-cancellable once production has started.

What does the warranty cover?

Manufacturing defects only. It doesn't cover damage from misuse, accidents or improper handling.

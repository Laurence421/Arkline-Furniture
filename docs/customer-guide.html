What your customers see, and how to try it

A walk through the website the way a customer uses it, from browsing your projects to sending an order. Try each step on your own phone; anything you send arrives in the admin page as a real request.

Test website (dry run): arklineofficefurniture.pages.dev
Your own address (for example arklinefurniture.com) replaces it when the domain is bought. Everything else stays the same.

Step 1

The home page

  1. Customers land on a full-screen banner of your best work, with your location and two buttons: View our projects and Free consultation.

  2. The ☰ button (top right) opens the menu: Projects, Shop, Services, About, FAQ and Contact.

  3. The clipboard button with a number is the customer's order list. It counts the items and liked photos they've picked.

Step 2

Browsing your projects

  1. Customers pick a category (Workstation, Conference Table, Operable Wall and so on) to see only those photos. The number shows how many photos each has.

  2. Tapping a photo opens it full screen. They swipe or use the arrows to see more.

  3. I want one like this adds that photo to their request, so you see exactly what they mean.

  • Every photo carries the see-through Arkline logo, so competitors can't reuse it unmarked.
  • New photos you upload in the admin page appear here within about 30 seconds.
Step 3

Shopping for chairs and tables

  1. In Shop, customers switch between Ergonomic chairs and Office tables, see the price and features, and tap Add to order.

  2. Prices come from the Prices tab in the admin page. Change a price there and it changes here.

  1. The order list shows each item with − / + for quantity, the liked photos, and the estimated total.

  2. It stays on their phone even if they close the page and come back later.

  3. Continue to order takes them to the form.

Step 4

Sending an order or asking for a price

  1. The form changes by itself: Send my order when shop items are picked, Ask for a price for custom work or liked photos.

  2. Customers give their name, a mobile number or email, the project type, and how they want to be contacted: call or text, Viber, Messenger or email.

  3. They can attach a photo or sketch of their space.

  4. If something is missing or mistyped (like a short mobile number), the form says exactly what to fix.

  1. After sending, the customer gets a request number (like #754) and the promise of a reply within one business day.

  2. At the same moment the request appears in the admin page under Requests, with buttons to call, text, Viber, Messenger or email them.

Step 5

Contacting you directly

  1. The Contact section shows your address, mobile numbers and email. On a phone, tapping a number starts a call and tapping the email opens their email app. Copy copies it.

  2. Messenger and Viber buttons appear once your Facebook page and Viber number are filled in under Website text in the admin page.

Dry run

Try it yourself: checklist

Do these on your own phone, then open the admin page to see what arrived. Tell us anything that looks wrong or confusing.

  • Open the test website. The banner, projects and prices load.
  • Pick a category, open a photo, tap I want one like this.
  • In Shop, add one chair and one table. Change a quantity in the order list.
  • Send the form with your real name and number, marked "TEST" in the message. You get a request number.
  • Open arklineofficefurniture.pages.dev/admin, sign in, and find your test request under Requests.
  • Tap a phone number in Contact. Your phone offers to call it.
  • When done, move your test requests to Trash in the admin page.

Prices, photos and text on this test website are the real ones. Change them in the admin page and the test website updates within about 30 seconds.

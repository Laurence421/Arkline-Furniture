What your website does for Arkline

A short guide to why the website exists, what it does for the business every day, and how to get the most from it.

The purpose in one sentence

Turn people who are looking for furniture into orders and price requests, day and night, without extra staff.

Your Facebook page and phone bring people to you. The website is where they see your work, check prices, and send you a clear, complete request that the team can answer in one tap.

Six jobs the website does for you

1

Shows your work

Your finished offices, workstations, kitchens and partitions in one organised gallery. Customers trust what they can see, and it's one link to send instead of dozens of photos.

2

Sells ready-made items

Chairs and office tables with clear prices. Customers add them to an order and send it, so simple sales happen without back-and-forth about price.

3

Brings in custom projects

A customer taps "I want one like this" on a photo, adds their size or number of seats, and asks for a price. You see exactly what they mean.

4

Saves the team time

Every request arrives complete: name, contact, items, photos and how they want to be contacted. An email alert tells you, and you reply by call, Viber, Messenger or a ready-written Gmail.

5

Keeps a record of every lead

Requests don't get lost in chats. Each has a number, a status (New, Contacted, Price sent, Closed) and private notes, and you can download them all to Excel.

6

You control it, no developer needed

Change prices, add new projects from your phone, hide items, edit the text and FAQs yourself. Changes show within 30 seconds.

How to get the most from it

  • Share the link everywhere: Facebook page "Website" field and pinned post, Messenger welcome message, Viber, business cards, quotations and delivery receipts.
  • Add new projects every month. Fresh photos show you're busy and give customers more to say "I want one like this" to.
  • Set "starts at" prices for common work like workstations and cubicles, so customers know if it fits their budget.
  • Reply within one business day. The website promises it, and fast replies win orders.
  • Keep prices up to date in the admin page, and download the requests backup every Friday.
  • Create a free Google Business Profile with the website link, so people searching "office furniture Novaliches" find you.
